At A Glance
Outcome Portfolio
Every card carries its own method badge — Deliverable cards show a real traced cost, Generated Artifact cards show an average, since one AI call can produce several artifacts at once with no honest way to split that call's cost between them. Click any card for the full breakdown and contributing calls.
Release Plan's own card leads with Direct Cost; its Economic Footprint (including upstream Discovery Map, Requirement Brief, Capability, Feature, and Story cost) is shown as a secondary figure and excluded from this sort and from Portfolio's totals above, since those dollars are already counted against their own outcome types elsewhere on this screen.
Abandoned Deliverable Cost
Cost of outcomes that were started and abandoned before completion, deliverable types only. Generated Artifact types have no abandonment state, a failed generation call there shows up as a worse avg cost per unit on its own card instead, not here.
Reporting period governs every section above. Outcomes are counted in the period where they reach their terminal state — completed or abandoned — not where they started.
